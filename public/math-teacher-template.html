Bio & Teaching Philosophy

A brief overview of my career, degrees, and guiding educational principles.

I am a passionate mathematics educator with over twelve years of classroom experience across secondary and undergraduate levels. My academic training focuses on differential equations and applied statistics, with a pedagogical emphasis on conceptual mastery and problem-solving resilience.

I believe that every student has the innate capacity to understand advanced mathematics. Too often, learners encounter math as a set of disconnected algorithmic recipes to be memorized for a test and promptly forgotten. In my classroom, we reverse that paradigm: we begin with physical intuition, visual geometry, and concrete questions before introducing algebraic formalism.

"We do not teach formulas to be recited; we cultivate minds that question assumptions, verify hypotheses, and see beauty in logical deduction."

Beyond the blackboard, I mentor our school's Math Olympiad team, organize weekly peer problem-solving circles, and develop open-source interactive graphing worksheets.

Classes & Courses

Course descriptions, meeting times, and core topics for the current term.

MATH 301 · Grades 11–12 · Period 2

AP Calculus BC

An intensive, college-level study of rates of change, accumulation, and infinite sequences and series. Prepares students for the College Board AP exam.

Core Units Covered:
  • Limits, Continuity & Differentiation Rules
  • Riemann Sums & Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
  • Parametric, Polar & Vector Functions
  • Taylor & Maclaurin Power Series
Prereq: Pre-Calculus (B+ or above) Credits: 1.0
MATH 202 · Grades 10–12 · Period 4

Linear Algebra & Vectors

An exploration of vector spaces, matrix transformations, determinants, and eigenvalues with applications in modern computer graphics and scientific computing.

Core Units Covered:
  • Systems of Linear Equations & Gaussian Elimination
  • Matrix Inverses, Rank & Subspaces
  • Linear Transformations & Projections
  • Eigenvalues, Eigenvectors & Diagonalization
Prereq: Algebra II Credits: 1.0
MATH 205 · Grades 10–11 · Period 5

Statistics & Probability

Introduces empirical data analysis, probability distributions, random variables, hypothesis testing, and confidence intervals using real-world datasets.

Core Units Covered:
  • Descriptive Statistics & Data Visualization
  • Normal, Binomial & Poisson Distributions
  • Central Limit Theorem & Sampling Error
  • T-tests, Chi-square & Linear Regression
Prereq: Algebra I & Geometry Credits: 1.0
